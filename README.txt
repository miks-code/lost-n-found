# FindIt – ICCT Lost & Found

Open `index.html` in a browser.

Files:
- index.html – homepage, about, login/sign-up overlay, and demo system pages
- style.css – responsive design
- script.js – navigation, login/sign-up, search/filter, logout
- assets/ – your uploaded campus background, logo, and item photos

The homepage is shown first. Login and Sign Up appear only after their buttons are clicked, while keeping the ICCT school image as the background.

Note: the login is currently a front-end demo. For a real XAMPP/MySQL system, connect the forms to PHP/MySQL later.
